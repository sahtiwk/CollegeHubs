Privacy notice for CollegeHubs, an independent student project.

Updated 20 October 2026

Account and Google sign-in

CollegeHubs stores your account email, username, profile details, college membership and sign-in records. Password accounts store a password hash. If you use Google sign-in, we use Google's account identifier, email address, email-verification status and, when supplied, Workspace domain to identify your account and check eligibility. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or calendars.

Linking Google adds a sign-in method to your existing account; it does not merge accounts. A Google account alone does not establish membership of a supported college.

What you choose to save or share

The app stores the content and activity needed for its features: Hub posts and conversations, Study work, calendar entries, game results, learning preferences and progress, interview practice, and reward records. Information you post in shared spaces is visible to the members allowed into those spaces. Profiles and rankings expose relevant profile and progress information within your Hub. Avoid putting private information in shared posts or documents.

Interview resumes, answers and reports are associated with their owner's practice sessions. Learn can receive selected practice topics through the explicit handoff feature; it does not read your Interview resumes or answers.

Optional AI and external services

When enabled and selected, AI-assisted Interview practice sends the supplied practice context, such as resume text, job description and answers, to the configured provider, Groq, to generate questions or feedback. Provider-free practice is also available. Do not submit information you do not want processed by that provider. Generated feedback is practice guidance, not an employment decision.

Optional external progress features use the public handle you provide to retrieve public statistics. Resource and contest links take you to other services with their own privacy practices. Google processes sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Hosting and browser storage

Cloudflare and Render serve the application; MongoDB Atlas and Neon store its databases. These providers process requests and stored data to operate the service. Necessary cookies keep sessions working. Browser storage also holds settings and session-coordination information. Signing out ends the current session; it does not delete saved content.

Retention and requests

Saved records remain in the app's databases until removed through an available feature or an operator action. There is no universal automatic account-deletion schedule or self-service account-erasure flow in this release. Backups can contain earlier copies, and reward receipts preserve the history needed to prevent duplicate awards.

For questions or a request to access, correct or remove information, use the CollegeHubs support email shown on Google's sign-in consent screen. Please do not post passwords, private practice content or identity documents in public repository issues. The operator may need to verify account ownership before acting on a request.
