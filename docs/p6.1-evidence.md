## Limits

Scoring is prospective; legacy scores and inactive facts receive no retroactive credit. Study completion rewards participation, not solution correctness. External bonuses and future module rules remain unavailable in normal composition. Leaderboard queries join/sort the current college within a deadline; cursor pages can move when balances change. Explicit Refresh updates the card/rank; no polling or live-update guarantee is claimed. Free-host sleep may delay Study reconciliation. Earlier dependency/security/hosted-release limitations remain tracked in prior phase evidence and P11.
