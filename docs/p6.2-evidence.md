## Limits

Background refresh is request-triggered and can be delayed by free-host sleep. Users explicitly reread the cache; there is no push-update guarantee. Provider access and dates can change. Calendar dates are fixed sample data pending reviewed college information, not official CVR dates. Read limits are 100 events/contests. No hosted deployment or production-scale claim is made.
